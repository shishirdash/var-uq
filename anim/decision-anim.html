dashed circles on the pitch = Pašalić frozen at each moment. The verdict hangs on whether a touch happened at all.
Everyone is moving at once — the dashed offside line sits wherever the second-last defender stands, and after the graze the whole defense collapses goalward, dragging the line with it. That is why the verdict shows a second, frozen dashed line: the rule compares Pašalić to where the line was AT the graze, not where it ended up. The one thing that decides everything is the one thing no human in the frame can perceive.

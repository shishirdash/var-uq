## New findings: tier 1

- N1. The momentum equation is wrong as printed. Locator: 'Mass * Velocity = Force *
  time_duration'. Impulse equals CHANGE in momentum: Force x time = Mass x (change in
  velocity). As written it equates full momentum with impulse, and it contradicts the
  correct velocity-CHANGE calculation two lines below. A physics error in a physics
  post; the fact-checker's top fix. Suggested wording, prose not symbols: 'force,
  times how long it acts, equals the ball's mass times the change in its speed'.
- N2. The compression amputated the spine, not the fat. Four seats independently:
  - The accountability thesis (the cut 'What nobody will tell us': no published
    algorithm, thresholds, or error rates; the 2022 Ronaldo precedent; 'two different
    error types, no error bars either time'). Mentor: 'the post loses its spine...
    you amputated the argument, not the fat'. Fan panel: that line was their top
    paste-quote.
  - The scored predictions (predicted 1, sim said 8; predicted 0.2, truth 0.35).
    Peer critic: the proof this was real socratic learning, not retrofitted
    narrative. Thought-leader: 'the mechanism behind your retention claim'.
  - 'Same graze, different spot on the ball, different verdict. I think about that
    more than I'd like to.' The lay reader's emotional payoff; he called its loss
    the biggest regression.
  - 'socratic in both directions': the named pattern the thought-leader would have
    quoted; deleted.
  All four together are roughly 150 words. Restoring them costs almost nothing in
